בטיסה — the lifted hero in transit

Four reports, one structural cause: the lifted hero has no in-transit variant. It renders a flight as an ordinary hard now-event and pins the flight's own rail below the next event's heading. Every frame here is the app's real CSS; every number under a frame is measured in-page.

§3 · מה כדאי להוסיף — והמלאי המלא

Report 3 ("estimated time till arrival") and the owner's "endless possibilities". Here is the whole inventory of what the app can honestly say about a journey in progress with no network, from startsAt/endsAt and the two ends' zones. Everything marked OUT is out because the data does not exist — not because there was no room.

העובדה מאיפה שיפוט למה
נותרו עד הנחיתה (1:39 שע׳) endsAt − now, hoursPhrase IN The literal request, and the one number a passenger repeats out loud.
שעת נחיתה בשעון היעד shipped (transit.endTime) IN Already on the collapsed board; the lift keeps it rather than restating it.
הזזת השעון במילים (מזיזים את השעון שעה קדימה) deltaMinutes + hoursPhrase IN The owner's idea. The pill (🕐 +1 ש׳) is the glance form of this; a sentence is what the lifted state is for. Same number, one elevation up.
השעה עכשיו ביעד the destination zone + the clock IN Rides on the clock-shift line at no extra height, and answers "do I call home now?".
חלפו (3:07 שע׳) / אחוז now − startsAt LATER The rail already draws the proportion. As numbers they are a second, weaker copy of it (§3ג).
משך הטיסה כולו endsAt − startsAt LATER Interesting before you board, not while you are inside it.
נוחתים מחר / בעוד יום relativeDay LATER Real and derivable, but only fires on an overnight; add it when a red-eye fixture exists to test it against.
מה עושים בנחיתה (הבא בתור) the horizon, shipped IN Already the hero's second half. See §6's open question about its countdown.
שער / מושב / חגורת מזוודות — OUT Not in bookingSchema. A slot for a fact we never have reads as a broken screen.
איחור / מסלול / גובה / מהירות — OUT Needs a live feed. Rule 5: everything works offline for reads — a hero that quietly needs the network is the one surface that lies on a plane.
מזג אוויר ביעד — OUT Same, plus it is not "what do I need in the next 30 minutes".

§4 · לבנות על המצב המכונס — ההבטחה של ADR-0160

Report 4, and the file's organizing finding: the collapsed in-transit board reads better because it has an in-transit grammar — a teal live word (בטיסה), a כרגע · בדרך label, a נחיתה chip, and the rail directly under the route. The lift threw all four away and substituted the ordinary now-event's קשיח + עד. ADR-0160's thesis is "one object that gained elevation", so the fix is not new grammar — it is the same grammar, one elevation up, with the depth added underneath.

No rail, no בדרך, no travelling mark: a hire's middle is a held resource, not a leg between two places. The deadline is amber (time), not teal.

Three fixes and one exclusion. (1) The rail's mark is the event's glyph, so train/bus/ferry/anything re-badged works with no per-mode SVG — and note the app has no train or bus icon to reach for, which makes the glyph the only non-inventing answer. (2) The live word comes off the time profile: בטיסה for ✈️, the generic בדרך otherwise. (3) A same-day car hire leaves the journey grammar entirely and reads as a held resource with a return deadline. This is ADR-0163 §4's rule one surface over: the verb and the unit belong to the span's own mode. Note the hire also reaches the collapsed board today, where it says בטיסה with a plane — so the fix lands on both, and the collapsed board is where it is worse.

§6 · ההרכבה — וכמה מקום זה לוקח

Everything recommended, in one card, at both phone widths. hero-horizon-v1 measured the room: 826px at 390×844 and 622px at 360×640, after the lift variant's 8px insets. The fit question matters because a content-sized hero that overflows becomes a scroller, and ADR-0160 §8 chose content-sized deliberately.
