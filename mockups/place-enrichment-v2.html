This file puts enrichment into that card. The governing constraint is the owner's own, quoted in map.css: "only the notes themselves should be scrollable, everything else is locked."

Photographs are synthetic (no egress to upload.wikimedia.org) — real aspect ratios, composed sky-above-structure. Names, hours strings and credits are real spike data.

1 · A committed place · the summary earns a pinned block

Two rearrangements pay for it, and expanding is a mode change rather than growth — it becomes the same card an un-added research place gets, so there is one presentation, not two. Hours join the meta line as another tag — the row already wraps tags, so פתוח עד 17:00 costs 0px instead of the 19–43px a line of its own wanted. That buys the summary a two-line clamp directly under the identity, always visible, tappable to expand. Expanding borrows from the notes scroller, which is the flexible track — so nothing is lost, it is just further down.

What v2 changes about v1

  • v1 designed for the empty case and called it the card. The real committed card already carries an order counter, a lock, a rename, three meta tags, a notes section with its own header and scroller, one or two reference rows with settle pairs, a primary schedule action and a delete. Adding a 132px hero and a 3-line summary to that would have pushed a capped card past the canvas.
  • The card has exactly one scrolling track and it is the notes. Four of five grid rows are pinned by the owner's rule. So enrichment cannot simply be "another block" — it either joins the pinned set (and must be one line) or it joins the scroller (and changes what that section is called).

The principle this file proposes

  • Enrichment is for deciding; once you have decided, it compresses. A photo and a summary of a place you have already committed to, whose booking you hold and whose notes your group has written, is the least urgent content on that card. Hours are the exception, because they are the only enriched field that changes what you do.
  • So the committed card gets exactly two things — the badge becomes the photo (zero space) and one pinned hours line with an "as of". עוד בגוגל joins the foot beside שיבוץ ליום, where it is also the whole answer for the majority of places we know nothing about.
  • And the deciding card gets the hero and the summary, because it has no notes, no references and no schedule action competing for the same pixels.

The fork this file cannot settle on its own

  • A vs B: does the summary appear on a committed place at all? A says no — the badge photo and the hours line are the whole of it, and anyone wanting more taps עוד בגוגל. B puts the summary and credit inside the scrolling track above the notes, which costs no pinned height and keeps the content, but renames the section from פתקים to something like מה ידוע — and that is a change to a surface the owner specified personally.
