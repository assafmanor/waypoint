## Context

The owner, after a ⁦15:00⁩ boat tour was cancelled by its operator at ⁦13:51⁩ and the row offered only `עריכה` · `מחיקה`:

> I want to do a product, design, then building session on handling last minute schedule changes — how we make this as easy as possible for users to do these kinds of things, thinking what's inconvenient and is holding people back, what's taking too many steps etc. The journey should be as easy as possible, and adding things, canceling, moving things around etc. should be seamless.

ADR-0228 §6 fixed the report (`דילוג` is in `⋯` on an upcoming booking, the parking lot takes a commitment). This session asked the wider question the report opened, the way the handoff said to: **name the journeys, count today's steps on the running app, then decide what "seamless" means for each.**

Three things this table says that the handoff's hypotheses did not:

1. **Every expensive journey is a decision that exists on paper and not on the surface.** The Trip card's tappable time (0161 §7), the Tier-1 quick-add (0043 §3, 0025), Do-it-now (0027 §1), the day-level delay (0161 §10). This is the seed report's own shape — _"decided things that never met on one surface"_ — four more times.
2. **Two defaults are wrong at exactly the hour a change is made.** The ＋ opens at midnight; the shelf schedules into the morning. Both are `nextSlot` / `firstPositionFitting` read without a clock, on the one mode that has one.
3. **The cancellation's count is fine and its consequence is not.** Two taps take the tour off; then the day says nothing about what it freed, the parked card says the wrong word and does the wrong thing on tap.

## Decision

**A change made on the ground is one tap on the thing that changes, its default is _now_, and the day says what the change did.** Five rules, one per journey; every mechanism named is one the app already has.

### 1. The time on the Trip card is a button, and it opens the day's positions

`.wp-event-time` on `EventCard` becomes a **button** on every planned row of a live day — upcoming, now, and a passed-unmarked row — exactly as `button.bld-time` is on the Plan row (ADR-0161 §7; this is that section's own Trip-mode sentence, built). It wears `ValueToken`'s hairline chip (ADR-0177: _a value shown as text that opens a picker is `ValueToken`_; the Plan row already composes `tokenClass`) and `ValueToken`'s inset overlay meets the 44px floor without growing the when line. It is **not** a button on a done or skipped row (the chip is the undo there, ADR-0230), not on a read-only day (ADR-0029), and not on a row with no time — that row's ＋ is §3's.

The tap opens a `Sheet` holding **`DaySlotPicker`** — the same component, the same `dayPositions`, the same `positionOption` labels Plan uses at its three call sites — with three things Trip mode adds by passing props, not by branching:

- **`עכשיו · 13:51` leads the list on today** (`DaySlotPicker.now`, a prop the component already has and Plan never passes). This IS ADR-0027 §1's **Do-it-now** on a slipped row and the same tap on an upcoming one.
- **No position before the clock, on today.** Positions are narrowed with `narrowGapToNow` before they are listed — the derivation `DayJoinRow` already applies to the hole you are standing in, applied to the chooser too. A hole entirely behind you is not offered.
- **`שעה מדויקת…` is a Tier-2 scoped sheet, not the whole form** (fork F3): the `ScheduleSheet` that already exists in `DayView` for an idea — a `WhenField variant="day"` and one confirm — generalised to take an event, the way `GapFillSheet` became `SlotFillSheet` (ADR-0161 §6). Plan keeps routing its exact-time escape to `EventForm`; Trip's escape is the smaller room, which is what ADR-0025 says a Tier-2 edit in Trip mode is: _"an inline bottom sheet scoped to the one edit, then drop you back."_

A pick writes through **`verbs.update` → `applyGuardedUpdate`**, the write Plan's `pickPosition` makes: a soft row moves in two taps to any position on the day; a **hard row asks once** (ADR-0011) and moves in three, whatever the distance. The ±30 stepper in the band is untouched — it is a nudge, and the button is a move; ADR-0161 §7 drew that line.

**What it costs:** the when line grows by the chip's border (`ValueToken`, `4px 8px` padding on a `12.5px` mono line); measured in the mockup at 360 and 390 against the shipped span. The `⋯` sheet loses nothing — `עריכה` stays for everything that is not a time.

### 2. A cancellation's second tap is its last, and the day says what it freed

The count stays at two. What changes is what the two taps leave behind:

- **The freed time is stated.** `dayBlocks` measures a join only between two **leaf** groups; a cluster starts no join, so a hole before one is drawn as nothing. The gap _after_ a cluster is genuinely not a single fact (two things end at two times); the gap _before_ one has one start, `cluster.startMs`. The join before a cluster is derived from its earliest member (fork F9, recommended yes). One rule in `lib/day-joins.ts`, both modes at once — `dayBlocks` is the shared derivation, so Plan's chip and Trip's strip gain the same hole together, which `frontend/CLAUDE.md`'s day-surface rule requires.
- **The parked card says what a commitment is in, not what we did** (fork F1). ADR-0228 §6b left `דילגתם` on a parked booking for the device pass and named the cheap answer — a kind-aware label, never a status. Recommended: `לא מתקיים` on a parked **booked or hard** card, `דילגתם` on a soft one. "Not happening" is true whoever cancelled; "you skipped it" is not.
- **A parked card opens its sheet, and `שחזור ליום` is the first row in it** (fork F2). Today a tap **restores**, which on a cancelled booking is the wrong verb for the tap you make when you want the confirmation code for the refund — and the code is exactly what ADR-0223 promised one tap away. The card takes the idea's shape from ADR-0116 §5a — a `RowManageSheet` whose first action is the thing the tap used to do — with `שחזור ליום` first and `להזמנה` (booked) or `פרטים` (unbooked) second. It amends §5a's reason for keeping restore-on-tap: _"a skipped event still restores on tap, because it has a surface of its own — its day row"_; a parked event **has no day row**, that is what parked means. Cost: one tap on the un-skip path. Recommended for every parked event card, not only booked ones — a kind-gated gesture is the branch ADR-0228 removed.

Nothing new on the follow-up (the owner's _"refund, rebook, tell the group — is that a task? a note? nothing?"_): the booking's read already hosts tasks and notes (ADR-0174 §3), and the sheet above is what makes it one tap away from the card. A task written for you by a cancel is a decision about the tasks model, not about this surface; not taken.

### 3. `＋` on today is a quick add, and it lands on now

The day head's `＋ אירוע חדש` in Trip mode opens the **quick add** ADR-0043 §3 decided: a `Sheet` with a title field and one when-sentence — `היום · עכשיו 13:51 · שעה` — where the time and the length are `ValueToken`s, and one primary, `הוספה`. That is three steps: ＋, type, add. The time token opens §1's `DaySlotPicker` (`עכשיו` first, positions from now); the length token opens ADR-0036's duration presets. `עוד פרטים…` hands the draft to `EventForm` through the `formDraft` state the errand return already uses, so nothing typed is lost when the quick add is not enough (a place, a booking, a hard kind — all Tier-2/3, ADR-0025).

**The default is `עכשיו`** (fork F4): the start is now ceiled to the slot grid (`ceiledSlotStart`), the length is `GAP_FILL_MINUTES` capped by the room to the next row (`blockFor`), the kind is soft, the category unset — the same posture as Plan's shelf jot (`AddIdea`, ADR-0109 §11: a jot takes no category; category is captured when it matters). Rejected as the default: `firstPositionFitting` from now — "the first hole with room" is the right answer for an idea being placed and the wrong one for a thing you are standing in front of.

The same quick add is what a gap `+` and `SlotFillSheet`'s `אירוע חדש` open in Trip mode, prefilled at that slot (fork F5, recommended yes): three surfaces, one form, one prefill rule. Plan mode's ＋ and `שבץ` keep `EventForm` — building the day is its job.

**Two defaults are fixed under the same rule, not forked:**

- **The shelf's Trip-mode quick-schedule never offers a position behind the clock on today.** `scheduleDefaults` narrows its positions with `narrowGapToNow` before `firstPositionFitting` — the ⁦09:00⁩ at ⁦13:51⁩ was the absence of that one line. Tested at the derivation.
- **The head's ＋ prefill on today reads the clock** — it is §3 itself; on another day it stays `nextSlot`, where "after everything" is what a plain ＋ means (ADR-0161 §10).

**Not taken now** (fork F8, recommended not now): whether a **done** event whose end is still ahead frees its remainder — the day tour marked done at ⁦13:51⁩ still holds ⁦10:00–16:00⁩ in every gap derivation. `עכשיו` covers the journey that surfaced it (you add the café at now regardless of what the tour's box says), and "done frees time" touches `dayEvents` in every derivation both modes read (`dayBlocks`, `dayPositions`, the travel legs, the leave-by) — a change to a fact, which ADR-0159 §1 says both surfaces must take together and which wants its own measurement.

### 4. `החלפה` is left alone

Three taps, the right question, the right consequence (the displaced event is parked, the replacement inherits the slot). Counted so the ADR can say it, not to change it.

### 5. The day takes a delay: `מאחרים`

ADR-0161 §7 deferred it with its shape already named: _"a **day-level** control on an on-the-ground surface, not a property of whichever row you happened to open"_, and §10: _"the ripple already performs it; what is missing is a way to ask without moving one event first."_

**The control** is `מאחרים`, in the day head's footer beside `＋ אירוע חדש` (fork F6), on **today only** and never on a read-only day — the same `live` gate the band's verbs take (ADR-0228 §5a). It opens a `Sheet` whose body is a **`ChoiceGrid`** of delays — `+15 · +30 · +45 · +60 · +90`, the stepper's own sign, the unit in the sentence above them (fork F7) — over a sentence that says, before you commit, **what moves and what does not**: _"בכמה דקות? יזוזו 3 אירועים גמישים מ־16:30 ואילך"_, and under them _"Ichiran Ramen · 19:30 נשאר · הזמנה. מה שאחריו לא זז."_ A chip commits on tap, the way a `DaySlotPicker` row does: two taps, one toast, one undo.

**What moves** is exactly what the server's ripple would move (ADR-0011, `computeRippleSuggestion`): every **planned, soft** event of today whose start is after now, **up to the first hard anchor after now** — an anchor re-synchronises you; being late for the afternoon does not make you late for the table you booked, and not for the evening after it. Events inside a cluster move with their peers. Nothing else on the day is touched.

**The write** is `applyEventPatches` — _"these events, these starts, one undo"_, named for exactly this in ADR-0161 §7 — with `startsAt`/`endsAt` shifted by the chosen minutes through the same `slotFor` a pick uses. Soft only, so no gate; one `REORDER` dispatch, one undo descriptor, N persisted moves, all offline-capable through the outbox. No backend change: the ripple stays server-side for the overlap case and is not consulted here, because a delay that preserves gaps is not an overlap to close.

Rejected placements, each drawn in the mockup so the rejection is measured rather than argued: the **now marker** (ADR-0043 §1 made it a quiet reference deliberately below the live event; a 44px control on it is a second loud element on the tab, and "now" is not where "late" is said); the **hero** (it settles a transition; adding verbs there is ADR-0160's decision to reopen, and the day is where you go to change the day); the **next row's band** with the ripple bar asking on every later nudge (noise on every `+30` that meant one row).

## Consequences

- **Frontend only.** No schema, no new REST path, no backend change. Every write lands on an existing verb: `update`/`applyGuardedUpdate` (§1), `applySetStatus` (§2, unchanged), `create` (§3), `applyEventPatches` (§5).
- **Journeys after**, counted the same way once built: cancel **2** (unchanged) with the freed time stated and the code one tap from the card; move to any position **2** soft / **3** hard; add now **3**; swap **3** (unchanged); running late **2**.
- **Three ADRs stop describing things that do not exist**: 0161 §7's Trip-mode time button, 0043 §3's quick-add, 0027 §1's Do-it-now — each becomes true by this build rather than by a fourth restatement.
- **One new sheet (the quick add), one generalised (`ScheduleSheet` takes an event), one new day control (`מאחרים`).** Everything else is a prop passed or a gate widened: `DaySlotPicker.now`, `narrowGapToNow` at two more call sites, a leaf rule in `dayBlocks`, a label and a sheet on the parked card.
- **Tests pin the rules**: the picker never lists a position behind the clock on today; `scheduleDefaults` on today never returns a start before now; the delay set is planned ∧ soft ∧ starts after now ∧ before the first hard anchor, and a hard row is never in it; the join before a cluster equals `freeBetween(prev, earliestMember)`; the quick add's default start is now on the slot grid and its length is capped by the room; the parked card's label is kind-aware and its first action is `שחזור ליום`.
- **For the device pass**, not for a screenshot: whether `עכשיו` at the head of the picker reads as "move here" rather than as a heading; whether `מאחרים` in the head is found when the view has landed on now and the head is scrolled off (§5's placement fork is the one a phone in a hand should answer); the chip's border on the when line at Samsung's text scales.

## What rendering it found

Drawn in [`mockups/the-day-is-changed-where-you-stand-v1.html`](../../mockups/the-day-is-changed-where-you-stand-v1.html) on the app's real stylesheets and layout trees, both themes, 360×640 and 390×844; every number below is read off that page's DOM.

- **The time chip costs the row nothing — after one correction.** The first render grew every card face ⁦69⁩ → ⁦79px⁩ (the chip is ⁦27px⁩ where the span was ⁦17⁩). The face's height is set by the ⁦40px⁩ badge, not by its text, so the chip paints ⁦5px⁩ into the face's own ⁦14px⁩ padding above and below (`margin-block: -5px`) and the face measures ⁦69⁩ → ⁦69px⁩, a two-line title ⁦88⁩ → ⁦88px⁩, the title's width ⁦113⁩ → ⁦113px⁩. The same trade `button.bld-time` made on the Plan row.
- **The overlay needs ⁦8.5px⁩, not ⁦8⁩.** ⁦27 + 16 = 43⁩, one under the floor — the exact shortfall ADR-0199's Consequences measured on the Plan row's identical overlay and backlogged. ⁦8.5⁩ reaches ⁦44⁩; the build should close the Plan row's with the same half pixel.
- **`DaySlotPicker` paints Plan violet**, and it had to: every shipped host is Plan. Drawn side by side, the violet `עכשיו` row on a Trip-mode day is a rule-4 violation in one look. The mockup carries the `--slotpick-accent` override (`SlotFillSheet`'s mechanism), so the picker in Trip reads `--cta`.
- **The quick add's when-sentence wrapped at 360** because it said `היום` twice — once in the sheet's title and once in the line. Dropping it from the line: ⁦31.8px⁩, one line, at both widths.
- **Five delay chips did not fit at 360 as `15 דק׳ … 90 דק׳`**; `+90` scrolled off the edge of the `ChoiceGrid`. As `+15 … +90` with the unit in the sentence: ⁦5 / 5⁩ inside the sheet's width, each ⁦54×44px⁩.
- **`.choice-pill` ships at ⁦36px⁩** (ADR-0052 §6, known); the delay chooser's pills are lifted to the floor in their own consumer rule, as `.category-pills` already lifts its own.
- **A second button in the day head's footer costs the facts ⁦77px⁩** (⁦211⁩ → ⁦134px⁩ at 360; ⁦241⁩ → ⁦164⁩ at 390) and the footer stays one line at ⁦46px⁩. `לפחות 18 ק״מ` fits; a longer fact would wrap inside `.wp-dayhead-facts`, which is a grid and may.
- **F6b, the now marker, rendered its own refusal:** a control beside the ⁦19px⁩ chip lands under the rule at the marker's trailing edge, ⁦74×21px⁩, and the marker's whole box is ⁦21px⁩ — three short of half the floor. A control there is a redesign of ADR-0217's mark, not an addition to it.
- **Three shipped shortfalls this design inherits and does not cause**, recorded so the build does not "fix" them at one host: `.new-event-btn` is ⁦26px⁩ (both head buttons wear it), `.field input` is ⁦37px⁩, `.sched-confirm` is ⁦42px⁩. Each is a primitive with many hosts; the fix, if wanted, is in the primitive.

## Out of scope, and said so

- **The change feed** (handoff hypothesis 6): a delay or a cancellation made by one member reaches the others as a feed row nobody sees. Unmounted from Home pending its own spec (backlog, owner 2026-09-10). Last-minute changes are the case the group most needs to know about; it stays a separate decision unless the owner pulls it in.
- **The hero as a place to change the day** (hypothesis 5). ADR-0160's surface; the day is where you go to change the day, and §5 rejects the hero as the delay's home for that reason.
- **A `canceled` status.** ADR-0228 §6b's reasoning stands: a Prisma enum migration plus ~40 `=== SKIPPED` consumers for a distinction the day does nothing different with. §2's label is the cheap answer it named.
- **A follow-up task written by a cancel.** The booking's read hosts tasks; the sheet in §2 puts it one tap from the card. Whether the app should write one for you is a tasks-model decision.

## Alternatives considered

- **Put the move, the delay and the quick add in `⋯`.** ADR-0161 §7's own first draft, rejected there off the mockup (eight rows, a scrolling sheet). The rule that came out of it — _a verb goes on the object it changes if that object is on screen; a menu is what is left over_ — places all three here: the time on the time, the day's delay on the day, the add on the ＋.
- **Grow the ±30 stepper** (a long-press for a bigger step, or 60/90 chips). Rejected by ADR-0161 §7 already: _"an arbitrary delay is two taps on the number that is wrong, and a hidden long-press on a control that already works is exactly the kind of second path this section exists to refuse."_ §1 is those two taps.
- **Make the head's ＋ open `EventForm` prefilled at now.** Fixes the default and keeps the six steps. The count says the form is the cost, not the prefill.
- **A new "quick add" component with its own input row** beside `AddIdea`. Two jots. §3 is a `Sheet` over `Field` + `ValueToken` + the shared picker — the primitives, not a copy of the Plan jot, and not a second one-line form.
- **Ask the ripple bar on every later nudge**, so "running late" is `+30` → `כן` today. Rejected in §5: it makes every deliberate one-row nudge ask a question about the rest of the day.
- **A `canceled` status**, above.
- **Free a done event's remaining window now** (F8). Deferred with its cost stated in §3.

## The forks for the owner

Each has a recommendation; a correction is not a fork.

1. **F1 · the parked commitment's word.** `לא מתקיים` on a booked/hard parked card and `דילגתם` on a soft one (**recommended**) · `דילגתם` on both, as today · `בוטל`.
2. **F2 · what a tap on a parked event card does.** Opens its sheet, `שחזור ליום` first, then `להזמנה` / `פרטים` (**recommended**, ADR-0116 §5a amended) · restores in place as today · the sheet only when the card is booked.
3. **F3 · the picker's `שעה מדויקת…` in Trip mode.** The generalised `ScheduleSheet` — a `WhenField` and one confirm (**recommended**) · `EventForm`, as Plan does.
4. **F4 · the quick add's default position.** `עכשיו`, ceiled to the slot grid (**recommended**) · the first position with room from now.
5. **F5 · where the quick add is reached from.** The head's ＋, the gap `+`, and `SlotFillSheet`'s `אירוע חדש`, all in Trip mode (**recommended**) · the head's ＋ only.
6. **F6 · where `מאחרים` lives.** The day head's footer, beside `＋ אירוע חדש` (**recommended**) · a chip on the now marker · the hero.
7. **F7 · how a delay is chosen.** A `ChoiceGrid` of `+15 · +30 · +45 · +60 · +90` that commits on tap (**recommended**, ⁦214px⁩ of sheet) · a `ValueToken` duration and a confirm (⁦254px⁩, one tap more).
8. **F8 · a done event whose end is ahead frees its remainder.** Not now (**recommended**; `עכשיו` covers the journey) · yes, in both modes, measured first.
9. **F9 · the join before a cluster is stated.** Yes, derived from the cluster's earliest member (**recommended**) · keep the leaf-only rule.
